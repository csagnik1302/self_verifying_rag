1. PROCESS EACH PASSAGE INDEPENDENTLY.
   Examine every passage separately. Do not combine information from different passages.

2. PASSAGE INDEX.
   The "passage" field must contain the exact zero-based index shown in the passage label.
   [PASSAGE 0] → passage = 0
   [PASSAGE 1] → passage = 1
   [PASSAGE 2] → passage = 2
   Never invent, change, or omit a passage index.

3. SOURCE FAITHFULNESS.
   Every nugget must be taken from a contiguous span of its corresponding source passage.
   Do not generate information that is not present in the passage.

4. TEXT NORMALIZATION IS REQUIRED.
   Every returned nugget must be clean, readable text.

   If the source passage contains run-together words or missing spaces,
   you MUST restore the missing spaces in the nugget.

   For example:

   Source:
   "distribution ofmalicious deepfake audio or visual media targeting a candidate running for public officewithin 60 days"

   Output:
   "distribution of malicious deepfake audio or visual media targeting a candidate running for public office within 60 days"

   Similarly:

   Source:
   "H.R. 5586:Deepfakes Accountability Act"

   Output:
   "H.R. 5586: Deepfakes Accountability Act"

   The nugget must never contain obvious run-together words, missing
   spaces, duplicated spaces, or malformed spacing.

   You may modify whitespace and obvious formatting artifacts for this
   purpose, but you MUST NOT change the underlying words, meaning, facts,
   entities, dates, or claims.

   Do not paraphrase, summarize, reorder, or add information.

5. ATOMICITY.
   Each nugget must represent ONE distinct and independently useful piece of information that directly contributes to answering the query.

6. FINE-GRAINED EXTRACTION.
   Extract nuggets at the smallest useful granularity.
   Prefer a short phrase or clause when it expresses one complete piece of information.
   Do not extract an entire sentence when only part of the sentence contains the relevant information.

7. SOFT LENGTH CHECK.
   Nuggets should generally be concise and preferably shorter than a typical
   sentence. As a soft guideline, prefer nuggets of roughly 5–20 words when
   possible.

   This is NOT a strict word limit. A nugget may be longer than 20 words when
   additional text is necessary to preserve one complete, self-contained fact.

   Do not shorten or split a nugget merely to satisfy the length guideline if
   doing so would remove necessary context or separate the components of a
   single indivisible fact.

   Conversely, if a nugget is unusually long, reconsider whether it contains
   multiple independent facts or answer components that should be separated.

8. SPLIT MULTIPLE FACTS.
   If a sentence contains multiple independent facts, extract them as separate nuggets.

   For example:

   "The EU AI Act was adopted in 2024 and entered into force on 1 August 2024."

   should produce:

   [
       "The EU AI Act was adopted in 2024",
       "entered into force on 1 August 2024"
   ]

   rather than returning the entire sentence as one nugget.

9. SPLIT LISTS.
   If a passage contains a list of independently relevant entities, laws, events,
   technologies, organizations, or other answer components, normally extract
   each relevant item as a separate nugget.

   For example:

   "Relevant legislation includes the EU AI Act, the GDPR, and the Digital Services Act."

   should produce:

   [
       "the EU AI Act",
       "the GDPR",
       "the Digital Services Act"
   ]

   Do NOT return the entire list as one nugget merely because all items occur
   within the same sentence.

10. DO NOT OVER-SPLIT A SINGLE FACT.
    Do not separate the components of one indivisible fact.

    For example:

    "The EU AI Act entered into force on 1 August 2024."

    should remain one nugget:

    "The EU AI Act entered into force on 1 August 2024."

    Do not split it into:
    - "The EU AI Act"
    - "entered into force"
    - "1 August 2024"

    A nugget may therefore exceed the soft length guideline when necessary
    to preserve a complete fact.

11. QUERY RELEVANCE.
    Extract only information that directly contributes to answering the query.
    Do not extract information merely because it is topically related.

12. CONTIGUOUS SOURCE SPAN.
    A nugget must correspond to one contiguous span of its source passage.
    Do not construct a nugget by joining words or phrases from separate parts
    of the passage.

13. COMPLETE INFORMATION.
    If a short phrase would lose the meaning of the relevant fact, include enough
    surrounding text to preserve the complete fact.

    Completeness takes priority over the soft length guideline.

14. MULTIPLE NUGGETS PER PASSAGE.
    A passage may contain zero, one, or multiple nuggets.

15. EMPTY PASSAGES.
    If a passage contains no information relevant to the query, return that
    passage with an empty "nugget" list.

16. OUTPUT COVERAGE.
    Return exactly one object for every passage provided in PASSAGE_LIST.
    Preserve the original passage order.

17. OUTPUT FORMAT.
    Return only the structured JSON output.
    Do not provide explanations, reasoning, commentary, or additional text.

18. FINAL TEXT CHECK.
    Before returning the output, inspect every nugget for obvious textual artifacts.

    Every nugget must:
    - have normal word spacing;
    - contain no accidentally concatenated words;
    - contain no missing spaces after punctuation where a space is required;
    - contain no duplicated spaces;
    - preserve the original words and meaning.

    If a nugget contains a spacing artifact present in the source passage,
    correct it before returning the nugget.
